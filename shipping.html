HOW WHOLESALE WORKS

Built around your order.

Wholesale purchasing is different from buying a single retail item. Product choice, quantity, availability, destination and shipping arrangements all help determine the right approach for an order.

At Trade Hub, buyers can explain what they need and discuss the relevant options with our team before moving forward. For larger requirements, container-based supply can also be discussed where suitable.

  • Tell us the product category and items you want.
  • Share your expected quantity or target order size.
  • Discuss minimum order quantity (MOQ) and availability.
  • Confirm destination and shipping requirements.
  • Review the agreed order details before proceeding.

WHOLESALE QUESTIONS

Before you enquire.

Here are a few useful details for buyers considering a wholesale requirement with Trade Hub.

Does Trade Hub display public retail prices?

This website is designed for wholesale enquiries rather than public retail pricing. Contact us with the product you are interested in, the estimated quantity and your destination to discuss the relevant wholesale details.

What does MOQ mean?

MOQ means minimum order quantity. It is the minimum quantity applicable to a particular product or order. MOQ can vary by product and supply conditions, so please confirm it with our team before making a purchasing decision.

Can I enquire about more than one product category?

Yes. Include all the product categories you are interested in and, if possible, the quantities you have in mind. This helps us understand your requirements more clearly.

Can I discuss container-based wholesale supply?

You can enquire about larger and container-based requirements. The feasibility, product mix, quantities, availability and shipping arrangements need to be reviewed for your particular order.

How much will international shipping cost?

Shipping costs depend on the destination, shipment size, product type, shipping method and agreed terms. Share your destination and estimated order details so the relevant arrangements can be discussed.

What information should I send with my enquiry?

Include the product or category, estimated quantity, destination country, any preferred specifications and your business contact details. If you are still exploring, simply tell us what you are looking for.
