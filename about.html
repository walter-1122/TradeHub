Trade Hub is a Dubai and Sharjah based wholesale trading company with more than 10 years of experience connecting businesses with products from the UAE.

OUR STORY

From the UAE to
markets worldwide.

Trade Hub operates across Dubai and Sharjah, two of the UAE's key commercial and wholesale hubs. Our business is built around dependable sourcing, clear communication and long-term relationships.

We supply a broad range of wholesale categories including perfumes, junior and adult trousers, ladies' premium bags, formal shoes, sneakers, skincare and makeup.

Over the years, we have developed business relationships and supported deliveries into markets including Oman, Pakistan and Saudi Arabia, along with other international destinations.

For larger requirements, our container-based supply model is designed to support serious wholesale buyers and businesses looking for consistent UAE sourcing.
