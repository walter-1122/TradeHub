WHO WE ARE

Rooted in the UAE.

Trade Hub operates with a focus on wholesale trading from Dubai and Sharjah. These locations form an important part of the UAE's commercial landscape and provide a base for connecting buyers with product sourcing opportunities.

We understand that every business has different priorities. Some buyers are exploring new product categories, while others need to plan larger orders around quantity, availability and destination requirements.

02 / WHAT WE DO

WHOLESALE SOURCING

Products and supply
for business buyers.

Trade Hub works across multiple wholesale categories, helping buyers discuss their product requirements through one business contact. Our categories include fragrances, junior and adult trousers, ladies' bags, formal shoes, sneakers, skincare and makeup.

The wholesale process begins with the buyer's preferred category, product requirements and intended order quantity. Minimum order quantities, availability and other order details should be confirmed for the specific products being considered.

For larger-volume requirements, container-based supply can be discussed according to the products, quantities, destination and agreed commercial arrangements.

Product specifications and availability can vary. We encourage buyers to share clear requirements so that the relevant details can be discussed before proceeding.

03 / GLOBAL REACH

INTERNATIONAL TRADE

Local connections.
International ambitions.

Wholesale trade extends beyond a single location. Buyers need to consider product availability, order size, destination, shipping arrangements and the practical requirements of moving goods across borders.

Trade Hub has developed business relationships and supported deliveries into markets including Oman, Pakistan and Saudi Arabia, alongside other international destinations.

Every destination can have different shipping, documentation and import requirements. These details must be reviewed for the particular order and destination rather than assumed to be the same for every shipment.

Our focus is to help buyers begin that conversation with a clear understanding of their requirements and the arrangements that need to be confirmed.

04 / WORKING WITH BUYERS

CLEAR COMMUNICATION

Every order starts
with a conversation.

A successful wholesale enquiry starts with the right information. Buyers can tell us which products they are interested in, the quantity they have in mind, their destination and any particular requirements for their business.

From there, the relevant product details, minimum order quantity, availability and possible shipping arrangements can be discussed. Pricing and terms should be confirmed directly for the specific enquiry before an order is agreed.

This approach helps establish realistic expectations from the beginning and gives both sides a clearer basis for discussing a potential business relationship.
